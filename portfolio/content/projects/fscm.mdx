## Problem

Running a sports complex means coordinating facilities, events, and equipment across different kinds of staff — and most of that coordination lives in spreadsheets and group chats. FSCM was built to give the complex a single system for it.

## What I built

FSCM manages sports facilities, events, and equipment with CRUD operations and role-based dashboards. It pairs a responsive React frontend with REST APIs over a PostgreSQL database.
