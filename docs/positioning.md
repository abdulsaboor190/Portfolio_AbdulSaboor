## 3. Recommendation

- **Headline: A** — "I build full-stack products that think with AI."
  It names both halves of the lead in one clause, sounds like a person
  talking, and leaves room for the bento grid to prove it visually.
- **Pitch: A** — it mirrors the headline's claim with the actual stack
  (React/Next/Node/Postgres) and the flagship proof (adaptive traffic
  control). Pitch B's proof points are stronger but say nothing about AI;
  Pitch C is safe but generic. If you want the internship front and
  center, B is the fallback — but the traffic system is the more
  differentiating proof.

## 5. Words to Avoid

Never use on the site:

- passionate, passional
- results-driven, driven
- dynamic, versatile, seasoned
- cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, innovative
- ninja, guru, rockstar, wizard
- go-getter, self-starter (as a trait claim)
- outside the box, think outside
- leverage, spearheaded, orchestrated
- "turned passion into" / "journey" framing

Replace with specifics: what was built, what stack, who it was for, what
happened. Numbers and project names do the persuading — adjectives don't.
